The mean across all units needs a weighted calculation

Each batch mean represents a different number of units.

BatchUnitsMean cycle time
A2010 s
B8020 s

The simple mean of the two batch means is 15 s.

The mean across all 100 units is 18 s.

(20 × 10 + 80 × 20) ÷ 100 = 18 s
